MECHANISM, NOT A PICTURE

What counts as discovery?

A new group is useful only if its training-induced interpretation predicts unobserved causal changes better than equally informed simpler explanations. Its visible shape or cluster coherence is not sufficient.

NO SEMANTIC CLAIM

Known toy dynamics

All training and test stimuli live in one predetermined Kirchhoff lattice, with a fixed number of possible edges. The clustering sees three supervised training probes; its category names are retrospective summaries. This does not replicate Goodfire's learned neural representations.
