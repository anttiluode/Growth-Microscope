NEGATIVE RESULTS COUNT

When simplicity wins

Early stopping and diversity can help, but aren't guaranteed to beat an equal-material uniform network on unseen endpoints. A causal fingerprint isn't better merely because it involved interventions: test it against material and training-flow baselines.

STILL NOT A NEURAL EXPLANATION

Known physics and region labels

Fixed rectangular region boundaries, prescribed Kirchhoff dynamics, supervised demands and deterministic runs. Nothing here proves Goodfire-style clean semantic circuits, neuron birth or transferable mechanisms across unrelated task families.
